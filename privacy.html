Keply

Privacy policy

Last updated 08.10.2026

Keply - Receipts & Warranties keeps your records on your own device. There is no Keply account, no Keply server and no cloud service, and we do not collect any data from you.

What the app stores

Warranty records, purchase dates, prices, notes, contacts, receipts, warranty papers, product photos, business cards and scanned documents are saved only in the app's private storage on your Android phone, iPhone or Windows PC. We cannot see them, and nobody else can unless you share them.

Camera and photos

Keply uses the camera only when you choose to photograph a receipt, warranty paper, product or business card, or to scan a document. It reads from your photo library or file system only the items you pick. Scanning happens on the device; there is no text recognition service and nothing is uploaded.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Keply schedules reminders on the device before a warranty expires. They are created locally and no information is sent anywhere. Keply may also re-schedule them after the device restarts. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your system settings.

Exports, backups and sharing

Backups (ZIP), PDF and CSV reports are files you create and keep yourself. When you share one, Keply hands it to your device's share sheet or file picker. Where it goes next (email, a messaging app, a cloud drive) is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy applies. Keply does not send anything on its own.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Keply has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not need an internet connection to work.

Deleting your data

Delete a warranty inside the app to remove it and its attachments, or uninstall Keply to remove everything it stored on the device. Backups and exports you already created or shared are not affected and must be deleted separately.

Children

Keply does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
